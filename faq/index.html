FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about PestMoto pest control software, the technician app, demos and getting started.

What is PestMoto?

PestMoto is pest control software that connects office scheduling and dispatch with a native Android technician app, automatic service records, payments and a customer portal.

Is PestMoto only an app, or is there an office system too?

Both. Office is the web workspace for dispatch, calendar, customers, leads, catalog, inventory, payments and compliance. The Android app is the technician field workflow.

How do service records get created?

Technicians complete a short end-of-service questionnaire covering work performed, findings, products used and customer notes. PestMoto uses those answers to build service notes, treatments, chemicals, history, compliance documentation and portal updates.

Can I download the technician app?

Yes. The Android APK is available at https://api.pestmoto.com/downloads/pestmoto.apk.

Do you offer a live demo?

Yes. You can open a populated Office demo at https://api.pestmoto.com/demo/ and a customer portal demo at https://api.pestmoto.com/portal/?demo=1.

Can PestMoto help migrate from FieldRoutes or GorillaDesk?

PestMoto can help bring supported operational data into a new workspace, including migrations from platforms such as FieldRoutes and GorillaDesk. Importable fields depend on the source data available, and migration is reviewed before anything is moved.

What is MotoGo?

MotoGo is a fast search-and-action tool inside PestMoto. You can find a customer, technician, lead, chemical or route and run an action without digging through pages.

How does a pilot work?

Start with a small team, run PestMoto alongside your current operation, and judge the workflow on a real route. Use the homepage pilot form to start a conversation.

Does PestMoto include payments and a customer portal?

Yes. Payment workflows can follow completed services, and the customer portal can present service history, billing information and Proof of Service when enabled.

Does PestMoto guarantee search rankings?

No. PestMoto is software for running pest control work—dispatch, technicians, records, and office ops. Marketing and search rankings are outside the product.
