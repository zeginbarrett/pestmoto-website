Service → Treatment → Chemical

1Service

The customer-facing plan or job: Quarterly Pest Control, Termite Treatment, Mosquito Service, Bed Bug Treatment, Exclusion, and more.

2Treatment

The work performed: web sweep, exterior spray, weep-hole injection, granulation, baiting, trapping, crack-and-crevice work, inspection, etc.

3Chemical / Product

The product tied to the treatment, including default rate, quantity, method, and inventory deduction.

Example: Quarterly Pest Control can include Web Sweep + Exterior Spray + Weep-Hole Injection + Granulation, with each treatment connected to its own product and application record.
